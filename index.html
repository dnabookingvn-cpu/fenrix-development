HOSPITALITY · FOOD · VIETNAM

BÚN MỌC TÂY HỒ.

Bún Mọc Tây Hồ is the first hospitality project being prepared for development under FENRIX DEVELOPMENT. Inspired by Vietnamese culinary traditions and built around the familiar flavors of bún mọc, the project aims to create a recognizable food concept with a strong identity and long-term development potential. This project represents the first operational foundation of the FENRIX ecosystem.
